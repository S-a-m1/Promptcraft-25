Year: 2087

After the Great Collapse of 2042, mega-corporations rose from the ashes of failed governments. Neon Nexus emerged as a sprawling metropolis built on the bones of old-world San Angeles, a monument to unchecked capitalism and technological advancement.

The Corporate Wars

Three mega-corps control the city: SynthCorp (cybernetic enhancements), NeuroLink (neural implants), and Grid Systems (infrastructure). Their cold war keeps the streets violent and opportunities abundant for those willing to risk it all.

Life in the Nexus

The city never sleeps. Neon lights illuminate endless rain-soaked streets. Hackers, mercenaries, and corpo-rats all fight for their slice of the pie. The strong survive, the weak get zeroed. Welcome home, choomba.

⚠ HOW TO NOT GET ZEROED ⚠

Essential tips for staying alive in Neon Nexus

01

Know Your District

Different areas, different rules. What works in Highrise will get you killed in Lowtown. Learn the local power structure before you make moves.

02

Trust No One Completely

Even your best choom might sell you out if the price is right. Keep your cards close and always have an exit plan.

03

Upgrade Your Chrome

Basic augments are the minimum. Get cybereyes for threat detection, dermal plating for protection, and a good neural processor for quick thinking.

04

Pay Your Debts

Borrowed eddies from a fixer? Pay them back on time. Interest rates are high, but the penalty for defaulting is higher - usually permanent.

05

Avoid Gang Territory

The Tigers, Claws, and Voodoo Boys all have their turf. Cross it uninvited and you're asking for trouble. If you must pass through, pay the toll.

06

Keep Your Net Secure

Netrunners can fry your brain through your neural link. Use quality firewalls and ICE. Cheap security is no security.

07

Never Trust A Corpo

They'll smile while stabbing you in the back. Every corpo deal has hidden clauses. Read the fine print or hire someone who can.

08

Carry Protection

Whether it's a blade, a piece, or just good combat chrome - never go unarmed. The streets are unforgiving to the defenseless.

09

Find a Ripperdoc You Trust

Cheap chrome installation leads to rejection, malfunction, or worse. A good ripperdoc is worth their weight in eddies.

10

Know When to Run

Pride is expensive in Neon Nexus. If a situation turns sour, extract immediately. Dead heroes don't collect payment.

11

Stay Off Corpo Radar

Once the big corps take interest in you, life gets complicated. Keep your head down, your profile low, and your work clean.

12

Learn the Slang

Talk like a tourist and you'll be marked as easy prey. Speak the language of the streets and you'll blend in with the locals.

STAY SHARP. STAY ALIVE. WELCOME TO THE NEXUS.
